WonderLab VR — Privacy Policy

Science lab for Meta Quest · Effective 19 November 2026

In short

  • No accounts, no sign-in, no names, no ads, no analytics, no tracking, no in-app purchases.
  • No online play, no chat and no links out of the app: nobody can contact a child through WonderLab VR.
  • We don't collect, store, sell or share any personal information. Progress is saved only on the headset.
  • WonderLab VR never contacts our servers. At most, Meta's own software on the headset confirms that you own the app.

WonderLab VR is a hands-on science lab for Meta Quest by Helpivy. You do the experiments yourself, with Touch controllers (swings, a water tank, magnets, gears and coloured light) while Curio, a friendly robot, asks you to guess first and then explains what happened.

Nothing a child does in WonderLab VR is ever sent to us. A child never types anything, and there is no name, photo or other personal detail anywhere in the app.

Cameras, microphone and sensors

The Meta Quest system tracks the headset and controllers so your view and hands move in the lab; WonderLab VR uses those positions only while you play and never records or stores them. It does not use passthrough or the headset cameras, the microphone, your location, contacts or files, or eye, face or hand tracking: it is played with Touch controllers. Curio's voice is recorded and comes with the app.

Network use

WonderLab VR doesn't need the internet, and it never contacts our servers or any analytics or advertising service. Copies installed from the Meta Horizon Store may ask Meta's Platform SDK on the headset whether the account owns WonderLab VR and, if the app is offered for mixed ages, which age group the account is in (Meta asks mixed-age apps to check this). Those checks are run by Meta under Meta's own privacy policy. WonderLab VR receives only the answers, keeps them in memory while it runs, and never stores or sends them. If Meta doesn't answer in time, you can still play.

Who it is for

WonderLab VR is for ages 10 and up: children of 10 and over (the youngest age Meta allows on a Meta Quest, with a parent-managed account) and their grown-ups. It is the same app for every age, with no ads, no purchases, no accounts, no online play or chat, and no links that lead out of the app. It collects no personal information from anyone, including children, so there is nothing for a parent to consent to.

Deleting data

Uninstall WonderLab VR to remove everything it saved on the headset. We hold no data about you or your child, so there is nothing for us to delete.

Changes and contact

If this ever changes (for example, if we add anything online), we will update this page and the date above before the change ships. Questions from parents, guardians or teachers: help@helpivy.com.
