Kissa — Privacy Policy

Android app com.helpivy.kissa and kissa.helpivy.com · Effective 9 November 2026

The short version

  • Kissa keeps a family's stories: voice recordings, optional photos and words. Only people you invite into your family can see or hear them.
  • No email, phone number or password. No ads, no analytics, no trackers, and we never sell or share your stories.
  • You can download everything, free, at any time, and delete everything from inside the app.
  • We never clone or imitate anyone's voice.

What we keep, and why

  • Names you type: your family's name (for example "Sharma family") and each member's first name or nickname (for example "Dadi"), so everyone knows who told which story.
  • Voice recordings you choose to save, and photos you choose to add to a story.
  • Words: a written version of each recording (see "Turning voice into words" below), questions your family writes, short voice replies and hearts.
  • A device key: a random code stored on your phone that lets it into your family space. We keep only a scrambled (hashed) copy of it, and the time it was last used.

Until you start or join a family, everything stays on your phone only.

Who can see it

Only members of your family space: the people who joined through your invite link or code. The person who started the family can remove members. Stories are never public. A story's QR code in a printed book plays the voice through a link made for that book. Anyone who scans it can hear that story, and see its words only if your family has checked them.

Turning voice into words

When a recording reaches our server, it may be sent to a speech-to-text service to make a rough written draft. Today this is Cloudflare Workers AI, which runs on the same Cloudflare network as our storage. If we change provider, this page will name the new one first. The provider processes the audio only to return the words; we do not allow it to be used to train models where the provider offers that choice. We never use your voice to create a synthetic voice.

Where it is stored

On Cloudflare (database D1 and file storage R2). Everything travels over encrypted connections (HTTPS). Your internet address is used for a moment to stop abuse (rate limiting) and is not stored with your stories.

Permissions

  • Microphone: only while you record a story or a reply, after you tap to record. Kissa never records in the background.
  • Photos: chosen through Android's own photo picker, so Kissa sees only the photo you pick.
  • Notifications (optional): only if you turn on the weekly reminder.

How long we keep it, and deleting it

We keep your family's stories until you delete them. In the app:

  • delete any single story you told;
  • Settings → Family → Leave this family removes you from it;
  • the person who started the family can choose Delete all our family's data, which permanently deletes every recording, photo and word on our servers within minutes.

Lost your phone? Use the form at kissa.helpivy.com/delete or email us and we will help.

Download everything

Your stories are yours. "Download everything" gives you all recordings, photos and words as a zip file. It is free, always.

Reporting

Every story and reply has a Report option. Reports go to your family's admin, and you can also email us.

Children

Kissa is made for adults and their families. It is not directed to children. Grandchildren can listen as part of a family, set up by a parent.

Third parties

No advertising, analytics or social SDKs. We share nothing with anyone except the service providers named above, who process data only to run Kissa.

Reading aloud

"Read this screen to me" plays recordings that come with the app. Names and words your family typed are spoken by your phone's own voice, on your phone. Nothing is sent anywhere to be read aloud.

Telling the family on WhatsApp

After something new is saved, the app may offer to share a link with your family group. It only does this if you tap Share, and you can turn the offer off in Settings. The link opens only for members of your family; anyone else sees nothing, not even your family's name.

One family across our apps on your phone

If you use more than one of our family apps on the same Android phone, they share your family sign-in (a random device key, your family, your language) with each other, so you don't have to join again in each one. Android only lets apps signed by us read it. Other apps can't. The websites don't share it.

Learning what helps

To learn whether the app is helping, our server counts a few things each day, per app: for example how many stories were recorded or games finished. Each count is only a number. No name, family, phone or internet address is stored with it.

In Settings you can answer "Is this helping?". We keep your answer with the app's name, its language and the day, plus only what you choose to add (words, a short voice note, or an email if you'd like a reply). It isn't linked to your name or family.

To stop abuse, we keep a scrambled (keyed hash) form of your internet address together with the day for one day only, then delete it.

Family home, new phones

So the family home can show a dot when something new is waiting, our server remembers when each member last opened each of our family apps. This is deleted when they leave or the family is deleted.

A family member can make a link to set up a grandparent's phone, or to move them to a new phone. Each link works once and expires. Every family member can see who made it, for whom and when. Moving to a new phone signs out the old one. Sealed letters written before the move stay sealed for 14 days.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here with a new date.
