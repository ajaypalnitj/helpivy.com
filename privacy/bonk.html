In short

  • No accounts, no sign-ups, no ads, no analytics, no tracking, no subscriptions.
  • We don't collect any personal information.
  • Progress and settings are saved only on your device.
  • Online play is optional and sends only the button presses, a made-up nickname, a random device code and the room code. There is no chat.
  • One optional one-time purchase in the Android app, behind a grown-up check, handled by Google Play.

About online play

Fight a friend on another phone with a room code. Online play is optional; everything else works offline. One player makes a room and shares its 4-character code (or a link with the code in it) with a friend. There are no public room lists, no matchmaking with strangers, no friend lists and no profiles — but anyone who has the code can join while a seat is free, so share codes only with people you know.

Nobody can type anything to another player. Each player appears under a nickname the game makes up (like “Bouncy Otter”; tap 🎲 for a new one), and our room server passes on no other names. There is no chat — only quick emoji reactions from this fixed list: 👋 👍 😄 😮 😂. The device code is random letters and numbers made by the game; it is not your phone's hardware ID or advertising ID. It lets a player who drops out get their own seat back.

Purchases (Android app only)

In the Android app, a grown-up can make one optional, one-time purchase (“All heroes”) that unlocks Luna, Coco, Ollie, Gizmo and King Grumble. It sits behind a grown-up check (a times-table question). There are no subscriptions, ads, coins or loot boxes. Google Play handles the payment and the grown-up's Google account under Google's privacy policy; we never see card details. The app asks Google Play whether this device's account owns the unlock and remembers the answer on the device. We have no server of our own for purchases. The website never sells anything.

Permissions

Internet (online play, and talking to Google Play about the optional unlock), Google Play billing and network state (both used by Google's purchase library), and Vibrate (little buzzes you can switch off in Settings). No camera, microphone, location, contacts or files.

Links out of the game

The “More games” page links to our other games on Google Play (in the app) or their websites (on the web), and to helpivy.com. It opens only after a grown-up answers a times-table question. The same kind of check comes before the optional purchase.

Children

Bonk Buddies is made for children aged 4 and up and their families. It collects no personal information from anyone: no names, emails, photos, voice or location, no ads and no tracking. Online, players only see each other's made-up nicknames, their moves and fixed emoji, and the game gives no way to type a name or a message, or to send a link or a picture. We built it with COPPA and the UK/EU children's privacy rules in mind.

Deleting data

Use “Reset everything” in the game's Settings to clear progress. Uninstalling the app (or clearing its storage in Android settings, or this site's data in your browser) removes everything it saved on the device. Online rooms delete themselves; email us a room code if you want one removed sooner.
