Chitthi — Privacy Policy

Android app com.helpivy.chitthi and chitthi.helpivy.com · Effective 9 November 2026

The short version

  • Chitthi turns a family's week (photos, drawings, voice notes) into one newspaper for a grandparent. Only your family can see it.
  • Parents post their children's news. Children don't have accounts.
  • No email, phone number or password. No ads, no analytics, no trackers. We never sell or share your family's posts.
  • Download any edition free, and delete everything any time.

What we keep, and why

  • Names you type: your family's name and each member's first name or nickname, and the first names of children a parent posts about.
  • Posts: photos, short captions, voice notes and "teach-me" items you choose to add, plus hearts and voice replies.
  • Your family's chosen day and time zone, so each edition arrives at the right moment.
  • A device key: a random code on your phone that lets it into your family space. We keep only a scrambled (hashed) copy.

Chitthi shares one family space with our other family apps, such as Kissa and Amaanat, so a family only joins once.

Who can see it

Only members of your family space. A printed edition's QR codes play voice notes through private links made for that edition.

Children's news

Chitthi is made for adults. A parent or guardian decides what to share about their own child and posts it themselves. Please share only what you are comfortable keeping within your family. Any member can report an item, and a parent can delete their posts at any time.

Where it is stored

On Cloudflare (database D1 and file storage R2), over encrypted connections (HTTPS). Your internet address is used for a moment to stop abuse and is not stored with your posts.

Permissions

  • Microphone: only while you record a voice note or reply, after you tap to record.
  • Photos: chosen through Android's own photo picker, so Chitthi sees only the photos you pick.
  • Notifications (optional): to tell you when your Chitthi has arrived.

Deleting

  • Delete any post or reply you made.
  • Leave the family from Settings.
  • The family organiser can choose Delete all our family's data, which permanently deletes everything.

Lost your phone? Use chitthi.helpivy.com/delete or email us.

Third parties

No advertising, analytics or social SDKs. We share nothing with anyone except Cloudflare, which runs our storage.

Reading aloud

"Read this screen to me" plays recordings that come with the app. Names and words your family typed are spoken by your phone's own voice, on your phone. Nothing is sent anywhere to be read aloud.

One family across our apps on your phone

If you use more than one of our family apps on the same Android phone, they share your family sign-in (a random device key, your family, your language) with each other, so you don't have to join again in each one. Android only lets apps signed by us read it. Other apps can't. The websites don't share it.

Learning what helps

To learn whether the app is helping, our server counts a few things each day, per app: for example how many stories were recorded or games finished. Each count is only a number. No name, family, phone or internet address is stored with it.

In Settings you can answer "Is this helping?". We keep your answer with the app's name, its language and the day, plus only what you choose to add (words, a short voice note, or an email if you'd like a reply). It isn't linked to your name or family.

To stop abuse, we keep a scrambled (keyed hash) form of your internet address together with the day for one day only, then delete it.

Family home, new phones

So the family home can show a dot when something new is waiting, our server remembers when each member last opened each of our family apps. This is deleted when they leave or the family is deleted.

A family member can make a link to set up a grandparent's phone, or to move them to a new phone. Each link works once and expires. Every family member can see who made it, for whom and when. Moving to a new phone signs out the old one. Sealed letters written before the move stay sealed for 14 days.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here with a new date.
