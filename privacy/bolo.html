Bolo — Privacy Policy

Learn to read Hindi, for Android (com.bacha.bolo) · Effective 19 November 2026

In short

  • No accounts, no sign-in, no ads, no analytics, no tracking, no in-app purchases, no chat and no online play.
  • Bolo has no internet permission, so it cannot send anything anywhere. Everything it needs ships inside the app.
  • Progress and settings are saved only on your device.
  • The optional "Record & compare" feature uses the microphone. It is off until you turn it on, and the recording is only played back, never saved or sent.

Bolo is a HelpIvy app that helps children read and speak Hindi (Devanagari), with words, sentences, a friendly guide called Sheru, stars and a daily streak. It works fully offline.

Microphone

"Record & compare" lets a child say a word or sentence, then hear the reference voice and their own attempt one after the other, so they can judge for themselves. It is off by default; it only starts after you switch it on in Settings and allow microphone access when Android asks.

When it is on, the microphone is open only while a lesson is on screen. The app keeps sound only during the child's turn, checks on the device that something was said (by loudness, not by recognising words), plays it back and then discards it. There is no speech recognition and no voice service: the audio never leaves the device, is never written to storage and is never sent to us or anyone else. The microphone stops whenever the child leaves the lesson or the app goes into the background. If you say no to the microphone, the app simply uses a tap-to-check step instead.

Network use

None. Bolo does not declare Android's internet permission, and the app is also built to refuse any request that isn't for its own bundled files. All words, sentences, fonts and voice clips (recorded in advance) ship inside the app, so nothing is downloaded while you learn and nothing is uploaded.

Permissions

  • Microphone (RECORD_AUDIO): only for the optional Record & compare feature described above.
  • Change audio settings (MODIFY_AUDIO_SETTINGS): Android's built-in web view needs this to route the microphone for Record & compare. Bolo does not use it for anything else.

Bolo does not use the internet, camera, location, contacts, photos, files or any device or advertising identifiers.

Device backups

If you have turned on Android's own backup in your phone's settings, Android may include Bolo's saved progress in your device backup, so it can come back on a new phone. That backup belongs to your Google account and is handled by Google; we never see it.

Children

Bolo is made for young children and their families. It does not collect personal information from anyone, including children, so there is nothing for parents to review or delete with us. There are no ads, no purchases, no links out of the app and no way for anyone to contact a child through it. We built it to meet the U.S. Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) and Google Play's Families policy by collecting nothing.

Deleting data

Use Settings → Reset all progress inside the app, clear the app's storage in Android settings, or uninstall Bolo to remove everything it saved on the device. We hold no data about you, so there is nothing for us to delete.

Changes and contact

If this ever changes, we will update this page and the date above before the change ships. Questions: help@helpivy.com.
