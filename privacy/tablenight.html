Table Night — Privacy Policy

Board games for Meta Quest · Effective 13 November 2026

In short

  • No accounts, no sign-in, no online play, no chat, no ads, no analytics, no tracking.
  • We don't collect, store, sell or share any personal information.
  • Settings, progress and a game in progress are saved only on your headset.
  • The only thing that goes online is Meta's own check that you own the app.

Table Night is a board-games app for Meta Quest by Helpivy: Carrom, Ludo, Snakes & Ladders, Chess and more, on your real table (passthrough) or in a virtual room, against computer opponents or with friends passing the headset.

Your room

In passthrough mode the headset shows your surroundings. The cameras are handled by the Meta Quest system; Table Night never receives, records or stores camera images or any map of your room. It does not use the microphone, your location, or eye or face tracking.

Network use

You don't need the internet to play. The only connection Table Night makes is Meta's licence check: when the app starts, it asks Meta whether the account on the headset owns Table Night. That check is run by Meta under Meta's own privacy policy; Table Night receives only a yes/no answer and keeps nothing. If add-on packs are offered later, buying them goes through Meta's store in the same way. We never send anything to our own servers.

Children

Table Night is rated for ages 13 and up, as Meta requires for Quest accounts. It does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children; it collects nothing at all.

Deleting data

Uninstall Table Night to remove everything it saved on the headset. We hold no data about you, so there is nothing for us to delete.

Changes and contact

If this ever changes (for example, if we add online play), we will update this page and the date above before the change ships. Questions: help@helpivy.com.
