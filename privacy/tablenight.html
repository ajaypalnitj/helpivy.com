Table Night — Privacy Policy

Board games for Meta Quest · Effective 19 November 2026

In short

  • No accounts, no sign-in, no online play, no chat, no ads, no analytics, no tracking, no in-app purchases.
  • We don't collect, store, sell or share any personal information.
  • Settings, progress and a game in progress are saved only on your headset.
  • The only thing that goes online is Meta's own check that you own the app.

Table Night is a board-games app for Meta Quest by Helpivy: Carrom, Ludo, Snakes & Ladders, Chess and more, in a cosy virtual room, against computer opponents or with friends passing the headset.

Cameras, microphone and sensors

Table Night does not use passthrough or the headset cameras, and never receives, records or stores camera images or any map of your room. It does not use the microphone, your location, or eye, face or hand tracking: this version is played with Touch controllers only. You never type anything into Table Night; player names are chosen from a fixed list.

Permissions

Internet, used only for Meta's ownership check described below. Nothing else: no camera, passthrough, microphone, location, contacts, files or hand tracking.

Network use

You don't need the internet to play. The only connection Table Night makes is Meta's licence check: when the app starts, it asks Meta whether the account on the headset owns Table Night. That check is run by Meta under Meta's own privacy policy; Table Night receives only a yes/no answer and keeps nothing. If Meta doesn't answer in time, you can still play. There are no in-app purchases today; if add-on packs are offered later, buying them will go through Meta's store and this page will be updated first. We never send anything to our own servers.

Children

Table Night is made for teens and adults (13 and over), and families often play it together by passing the headset. It has no online play, no chat, no ads and no links out of the app, so nobody can contact a player through it. It collects no personal information from anyone, including children.

Deleting data

Uninstall Table Night to remove everything it saved on the headset. We hold no data about you, so there is nothing for us to delete.

Changes and contact

If this ever changes (for example, if we add online play), we will update this page and the date above before the change ships. Questions: help@helpivy.com.
