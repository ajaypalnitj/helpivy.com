WonderLab — Privacy Policy

Science lab for children on the web and Android (com.helpivy.wonderlab) · Effective 19 November 2026

In short

  • No accounts, no sign-in, no names, no ads, no analytics, no tracking, no in-app purchases.
  • No online play, no chat, and no links out of the app: nobody can contact a child through WonderLab.
  • We don't collect, store, sell or share any personal information. Progress is saved only on the device.
  • The Android app has no permission to use the internet at all. The web version only downloads itself and Curio's voice from our host.

WonderLab is a science lab by Helpivy: twenty experiment stations with real physics, where a child guesses what will happen and then finds out, with Curio, a friendly guide who talks them through it. It runs in a web browser and as an Android app.

Nothing a child does in WonderLab is ever sent to us. A child never types anything: the level is picked with a tap, and there is no name, photo or other personal detail anywhere in the app.

Cameras, microphone and sensors

WonderLab does not use the camera, the microphone, location, contacts, photos or files. On Android its only permission is Vibrate, for small buzzes when something lands. On the web it never asks for any browser permission.

Network use

Android: the app has no internet permission, so it cannot connect to anything, ours or anyone else's. Everything, Curio's voice included, is inside the app.

Web: the browser downloads WonderLab and Curio's recorded voice from our host on Cloudflare, and keeps them so the lab works offline afterwards. WonderLab sends nothing back: there is no analytics, no tracking, no cookies and no third-party content (the fonts are our own copies). Like any website, the requests themselves reach Cloudflare, which handles them under Cloudflare's privacy policy.

Curio's voice

Almost everything Curio says is a recording that comes with the app. A few lines (for example, ones that read out a number from the experiment), and any recording that isn't available offline yet, are read by the device's own speech voice instead: the browser's built-in voice on the web, or the phone's text-to-speech engine on Android. Only WonderLab's own sentence is passed to that voice, never anything about the child. Some voices are produced online by the browser or phone maker (for example, some Google voices in Chrome); those are run by that company under its own privacy policy and its own settings.

Children

WonderLab is made for children, with two levels pitched at ages 6 to 8 and 9 to 12, and younger children are welcome to play alongside a grown-up. It has no ads, no purchases, no accounts, no online play or chat, and no links that lead out of the app. Because it collects no personal information from anyone, there is nothing for a parent to consent to, and it was built with COPPA, Google Play's Families policy and the UK/EU children's privacy rules in mind.

Backups on Android

If Android's own backup is switched on for the device, Android may include WonderLab's saved progress in the backup it keeps in the device owner's Google account, so progress can come back on a new phone. That backup is made and held by Google under the owner's control; we can't see it or access it.

Deleting data

Tap "Clear all progress" on the For grown-ups page (in the Notebook) to start the lab fresh. To remove everything, clear WonderLab's site data in your browser settings, or uninstall the Android app. We hold no data about you or your child, so there is nothing for us to delete.

Changes and contact

If this ever changes (for example, if we add anything online), we will update this page and the date above before the change ships. Questions from parents, guardians or teachers: help@helpivy.com.
