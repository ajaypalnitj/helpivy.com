About online play

Play Ludo or Snakes & Ladders with friends on other devices using a room code; the host can fill empty seats with bots, and the server rolls every die. Online play is optional; everything else works offline. One player makes a room and shares its 4-character code (or a link with the code in it) with a friend. There are no public room lists, no matchmaking with strangers, no friend lists and no profiles — but anyone who has the code can join while a seat is free, so share codes only with people you know.

Nobody can type anything to another player. Each player appears under a nickname the game makes up (like “Bouncy Otter”; tap 🎲 for a new one) and a picture from a fixed set, and our room server passes on no other names. There is no chat — only quick emoji reactions from this fixed list: 👋 👍 😄 😮 🎉 😂 🐍. The device code is random letters and numbers made by the game; it is not your phone's hardware ID or advertising ID. It lets a player who drops out get their own seat back.

Permissions

Internet (online play only) and Vibrate (little buzzes you can switch off in Settings). No camera, microphone, location, contacts or files.

Links out of the game

The “More games” page links to our other games on Google Play (in the app) or their websites (on the web), and to helpivy.com. It opens only after a grown-up answers a times-table question.

Children

Ludo & Snakes is made for children aged 4 and up and their families. It collects no personal information from anyone: no names, emails, photos, voice or location, no ads and no tracking. Online, players only see each other's made-up nicknames and pictures, their moves and fixed emoji, and the game gives no way to type a name or a message, or to send a link or a picture. We built it with COPPA and the UK/EU children's privacy rules in mind.

Deleting data

Use “Reset my trophies” in the game's Settings to clear progress. Uninstalling the app (or clearing its storage in Android settings, or this site's data in your browser) removes everything it saved on the device. Online rooms delete themselves; email us a room code if you want one removed sooner.
