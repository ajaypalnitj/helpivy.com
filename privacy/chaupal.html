Chaupal — Privacy Policy

Android app com.helpivy.chaupal and chaupal.helpivy.com · Effective 19 November 2026

The short version

  • Chaupal lets you play Ludo and Snakes & Ladders against the computer, with people beside you, or with family far away through a link.
  • No accounts, no ads, no analytics companies, no trackers. No scores or coins.
  • Online game rooms, and any voice notes sent in them, delete themselves after 3 quiet days.

Playing on your phone

Games against the computer or passed around the table stay on your phone. Your language, settings and an unfinished game are saved only on your phone, or in your browser on the website.

Playing with family online

When you tap "Play with family", we make a game room on our server and give you a link to share, for example on WhatsApp. Anyone with the link can open that room and see who is playing which game; while there are free seats, they can join. The room keeps:

  • the name or nickname each player types (like "Nani" or "Simran");
  • a random code made by each phone, so a player who drops off gets their own seat back;
  • the game: whose turn it is and where the pieces are;
  • preset phrases and emoji you send, and the last six short voice notes (up to 20 seconds each), if you choose to record one. Everyone seated in the room can hear them.

A room is deleted automatically, with everything in it, after 3 days with no one playing. We don't use rooms for anything else.

If your family uses our other family apps, Chaupal can show family members as one-tap invites. The invite (which room, which game, who it is for) is kept in your family's shared space, described in our Kissa policy, and the next invite replaces it.

Where it is stored

Game rooms run on Cloudflare, over encrypted connections (HTTPS and secure WebSockets). Your internet address is used for a moment to deliver the game and stop abuse, and is not stored in the room. Cloudflare keeps short technical logs of requests for a few days; when a phone opens a room these include the room's address, with the name the player typed.

Permissions

  • Microphone (optional): only while you record a voice note in an online game, after you tap to record.

Children

Chaupal is made for adults to play with their families, and is not directed to children. It has no accounts, no free-text chat (only preset phrases, emoji and voice notes) and collects nothing beyond what's described above. Anyone who has a room's link can join it while there's a seat, so share links only with family. A parent or guardian should supervise a child who plays online.

Third parties

No advertising, analytics or social SDKs, and no third-party trackers. We share nothing with anyone except Cloudflare, which runs the game rooms.

Reading aloud

"Read this screen to me" plays recordings that come with the app. Names and words your family typed are spoken by a computer voice. In the Android app that is your phone's own text-to-speech, on your phone. On the website it is your browser's voice: some browsers (for example Chrome on a computer) use an online voice from their maker, such as Google, which then receives those words to speak them. We send nothing anywhere to be read aloud.

One family across our apps on your phone

If you use more than one of our family apps on the same Android phone, they share your family sign-in (a random device key, your family, your language) with each other, so you don't have to join again in each one. Android only lets apps signed by us read it. Other apps can't. The websites don't share it.

Family and guests at the table

When a family member joins a game room through our family apps, the room gets a short-lived pass (12 hours) with the room code, a scrambled form of the family (never its id) and their name, so the room knows they're family. In a room a family member made through our family apps, anyone else who opens the link is shown to everyone as "Guest (not in the family)", can't send voice notes, and can be removed by whoever made the room. Guests can still hear the voice notes sent in the room while they are seated.

Learning what helps

To learn whether the app is helping, our server counts a few things each day, per app: for example how many games were finished. This is our own count, not an analytics company. Each count is only a number: no name, family, phone or internet address is stored with it.

To stop abuse, we keep a scrambled (keyed hash) form of your internet address together with the day for one day only, then delete it.

Family home

If Chaupal is connected to your family, our server remembers when you last opened it, so the family home can show what's new. This is deleted when you leave or the family is deleted.
