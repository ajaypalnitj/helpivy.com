Chaupal — Privacy Policy

Android app com.helpivy.chaupal and chaupal.helpivy.com · Effective 9 November 2026

The short version

  • Chaupal lets you play Ludo and Snakes & Ladders against the computer, with people beside you, or with family far away through a link.
  • No accounts, no ads, no analytics, no trackers. No scores or coins.
  • Online game rooms, and any voice notes sent in them, delete themselves after 3 quiet days.

Playing on your phone

Games against the computer or passed around the table stay on your phone. Your language, settings and an unfinished game are saved only on your phone, or in your browser on the website.

Playing with family online

When you tap "Play with family", we make a game room on our server and give you a link to share, for example on WhatsApp. Anyone with the link can join that room. The room keeps:

  • the name or nickname each player types (like "Nani" or "Simran");
  • the game: whose turn it is and where the pieces are;
  • preset phrases and emoji you send, and the last few short voice notes, if you choose to record one.

A room is deleted automatically, with everything in it, after 3 days with no one playing. We don't use rooms for anything else.

If your family uses our other family apps, Chaupal can show family members as one-tap invites. That uses the shared family space described in our Kissa policy.

Where it is stored

Game rooms run on Cloudflare, over encrypted connections (HTTPS). Your internet address is used for a moment to deliver the game and stop abuse, and is not stored in the room.

Permissions

  • Microphone (optional): only while you record a voice note in an online game, after you tap to record.

Children

Chaupal is made for adults to play with their families. It is not directed to children, has no accounts, and collects nothing beyond what's described above.

Third parties

No advertising, analytics or social SDKs. We share nothing with anyone except Cloudflare, which runs the game rooms.

Learning what helps

To learn whether the app is helping, our server counts a few things each day, per app: for example how many stories were recorded or games finished. Each count is only a number. No name, family, phone or internet address is stored with it.

In Settings you can answer "Is this helping?". We keep your answer with the app's name, its language and the day, plus only what you choose to add (words, a short voice note, or an email if you'd like a reply). It isn't linked to your name or family.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here with a new date.
