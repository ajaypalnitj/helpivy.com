Skyvault — Privacy Policy

Flight game for Meta Quest · Effective 13 November 2026

In short

  • No accounts, no sign-ups, no ads, no analytics, no tracking.
  • We don't collect, store or share any personal information.
  • Settings, best times and medals are saved only on your headset.
  • The only thing that goes online is Meta's own check that you own the game.

Meta's ownership check

When Skyvault starts, it asks Meta whether the account signed in to the headset owns the game. That check is run by Meta under Meta's own privacy policy. Skyvault receives only a yes/no answer and never sees or stores your Meta account details. We never send anything to our own servers.

Permissions

Internet, used only for Meta's ownership check. No camera, passthrough, microphone, hand tracking, location, contacts or files. You play with your Touch controllers.

Children

Skyvault is rated for ages 13 and up, as Meta requires for Quest accounts. It does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children.

Deleting data

Uninstall Skyvault to remove everything it saved on the headset. We hold no data about you, so there is nothing for us to delete.

Changes and contact

If this ever changes (for example, if we add online features), we will update this page and the date above before the change ships. Questions: help@helpivy.com.
