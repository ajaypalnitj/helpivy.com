Painti — Privacy Policy

Android app com.bacha.painti · Effective 3 November 2026

The short version

  • Painti collects no personal data and works fully offline.
  • No accounts, no ads, no analytics and no third-party trackers.
  • Progress is saved only on your device.

Information we collect

None. Painti does not ask for, collect or send any personal information: no name, email, phone number, location, contacts, photos or device identifiers.

Learning progress

Your learning progress (such as stars, daily streak, and which letters and words you have unlocked) is saved only on your device, in the app's local storage. It never leaves your phone or tablet. You can erase it at any time from Settings → Reset all progress inside the app, or by uninstalling the app.

Network and permissions

Painti does not need an internet connection and makes no network requests.

The app has one optional feature, "Record & compare", which uses your device's microphone so a child can record themselves saying a word and hear it played back next to the reference pronunciation. It is off by default. When it is on, the audio is processed only in your device's memory for instant playback. It is never saved, never uploaded and never shared, and you can leave it off entirely in Settings. Painti requests no other sensitive permissions (no camera, location, contacts or storage).

Children's privacy

Painti is designed for children and families. Because the app collects no data at all, it does not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13 (or the relevant age in your region). It was built with the U.S. Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) and the Google Play Families policy in mind, by collecting nothing.

Third parties

Painti contains no third-party advertising, analytics or social SDKs and shares no information with anyone, because none is collected.

Changes to this policy

If this policy ever changes, the updated version will be posted at this same address with a new effective date.

Contact

Questions about this policy? Email help@helpivy.com.

This policy applies to the Painti Android app (package com.bacha.painti). Our games have their own privacy policy.
