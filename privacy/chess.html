ChessQuest — Privacy Policy

Android app com.bacha.chessquest and chess.helpivy.com · Effective 2 November 2026

In short

  • No accounts, no sign-ups, no ads, no analytics, no tracking.
  • We don't collect any personal information.
  • Progress and settings are saved only on your device.
  • Online play sends only the moves, a made-up nickname, a random device code and the room code.

About online play

Play chess with a friend on another device using a room code. The server checks every move is legal. Online play is optional and needs a room code shared between friends. There is no chat and nothing can be typed to another player — only quick emoji reactions from a fixed list. Online, every player appears under a nickname the game makes up (never a typed name).

Permissions

Internet (online play only) and Vibrate (little buzzes you can switch off in Settings). No camera, microphone, location, contacts or files.

Links out of the game

The "More games" page links to our other games on Google Play and helpivy.com. It opens only after a grown-up answers a maths question.

Children

ChessQuest is made for children aged 4 and up. Because it collects no personal information, there is nothing for a parent to consent to, and it was built with COPPA and the UK/EU children's privacy rules in mind.

Deleting data

Use Reset in the game's Settings, or uninstall the app, to remove everything it saved on the device. Online rooms delete themselves; email us a room code if you want one removed sooner.
