Amaanat — Privacy Policy

Android app com.helpivy.amaanat and amaanat.helpivy.com · Effective 19 November 2026

The short version

  • Amaanat keeps voice letters that a grandparent records for someone in the family, sealed until the day or moment they choose.
  • Until a letter opens, our server gives its recording, photo and note only to the person who recorded it: not to parents, not to the family organiser, not to anyone else in the family.
  • No email, phone number or password. No ads, no analytics companies, no trackers. We never copy or imitate anyone's voice.
  • Save any opened letter to your phone for free. Delete everything any time.

What we keep, and why

  • Names you type: your family's name and each member's first name or nickname. For a child who hasn't joined yet, their name, and if you add them, their birthday and wedding date, which are used only to decide when a letter opens.
  • Letters: the voice recording, an optional photo and an optional short note, plus who it is for and when it opens.
  • Thank-yous: a short voice reply the person sends back after opening a letter. Only they and the letter's author can hear it.
  • A device key: a random code on your phone that lets it into your family space. We keep only a scrambled (hashed) copy.

Amaanat shares one family space with our other family apps, such as Kissa, so a family only joins once.

How the seal works

Our server will not give out a sealed letter's recording, photo or note to anyone except its author until the letter opens. Dated letters open on that date. "Open when…" letters open when their person chooses. Changes that could open a letter sooner, like setting a wedding date or handing a child's letters to them, need the grandparent's okay or a notice period they can undo.

Sealed letters are not sent for speech-to-text, and are never in a public link.

The seal is kept by our server, not by encryption on your phone. Letters are stored like the rest of your family's content (see below), so they are not hidden from the service itself. Neither the app nor the website gives anyone else a way to hear or see a letter before it opens.

Where it is stored

On Cloudflare (database D1 and file storage R2), encrypted on the way (HTTPS) and where it is stored. It is not end-to-end encrypted: our server can read it, because it has to sort and play it for your family. Your internet address is used for a moment to stop abuse (rate limiting) and is not stored with your letters. Cloudflare keeps short technical logs of requests (such as the internet address and the address of the page or file asked for) for a few days, and our database keeps automatic restore points for up to 30 days, so something you delete is gone from those too within 30 days.

Permissions

  • Microphone: only while you record a letter or a thank-you, after you tap to record.
  • Photos: chosen through Android's own photo picker or taken with your camera app, so Amaanat sees only the photo you pick or take; it asks for no storage or camera permission. Photos are made smaller on your phone before they are sent, which also drops details hidden in the photo file, such as where it was taken.
  • Notifications (optional): to tell you on the day a letter opens. Gentle in-app reminders (for example a birthday coming up) are shown at most twice; our server remembers which ones you have seen.

Deleting

  • Before a letter is opened, its author can record it again or take it back.
  • The person a letter is for can remove it after opening.
  • The family organiser can choose Delete all our family's data, which permanently deletes everything, sealed letters included.

Lost your phone? Use amaanat.helpivy.com/delete or email us.

Reporting

Every opened letter and thank-you has a Report option. You can also email us.

Children

Amaanat is made for adults and their families, and is not directed to children. A parent can add a young child as someone letters are for (their name, and if the parent adds them, their birthday and wedding day), and keeps those letters until the child joins the family space through an invite from the family. A parent or guardian should decide when a child under 13 joins, and stay involved. We don't knowingly collect information directly from a child under 13 without a parent's involvement; if you think we have, email us and we will delete it.

Third parties

No advertising, analytics or social SDKs, and no third-party trackers. We share nothing with anyone except Cloudflare, which runs our storage.

Reading aloud

"Read this screen to me" plays recordings that come with the app. Names and words your family typed are spoken by a computer voice. In the Android app that is your phone's own text-to-speech, on your phone. On the website it is your browser's voice: some browsers (for example Chrome on a computer) use an online voice from their maker, such as Google, which then receives those words to speak them. We send nothing anywhere to be read aloud.

One family across our apps on your phone

If you use more than one of our family apps on the same Android phone, they share your family sign-in (a random device key, your family, your language) with each other, so you don't have to join again in each one. Android only lets apps signed by us read it. Other apps can't. The websites don't share it.

Learning what helps

To learn whether the app is helping, our server counts a few things each day, per app: for example how many stories were recorded or games finished. This is our own count, not an analytics company. Each count is only a number: no name, family, phone or internet address is stored with it.

In Settings you can answer "Is this helping?". We keep your answer with the app's name, its language and the day, plus only what you choose to add (words, a short voice note, or an email address or phone number if you'd like a reply). It isn't linked to your name or family.

To stop abuse, we keep a scrambled (keyed hash) form of your internet address together with the day for one day only, then delete it.

Family home, new phones

So the family home can show a dot when something new is waiting, our server remembers when each member last opened each of our family apps. It also keeps each member's language and time zone, and, if the family says so, who is one of the family's elders and who is a parent or a grandchild, so each app speaks the right language and shows the right things. This is deleted when they leave or the family is deleted.

A family member can make a link to set up a grandparent's phone, or to move them to a new phone. Each link works once and expires. Every family member can see who made it, for whom and when. Moving to a new phone signs out the old one. Sealed letters written before the move stay sealed for 14 days.
