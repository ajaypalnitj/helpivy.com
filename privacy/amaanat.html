Amaanat — Privacy Policy

Android app com.helpivy.amaanat and amaanat.helpivy.com · Effective 8 November 2026

The short version

  • Amaanat keeps voice letters that a grandparent records for someone in the family, sealed until the day or moment they choose.
  • A sealed letter can be heard only by the person who recorded it, until it opens for the person it is for. Not by parents, not by the family organiser, and not by us.
  • No email, phone number or password. No ads, no analytics, no trackers. We never copy or imitate anyone's voice.
  • Save any opened letter to your phone for free. Delete everything any time.

What we keep, and why

  • Names you type: your family's name and each member's first name or nickname. For a child who hasn't joined yet, their name, and if you add them, their birthday and wedding date, which are used only to decide when a letter opens.
  • Letters: the voice recording, an optional photo and an optional short note, plus who it is for and when it opens.
  • Thank-yous: a short voice reply the person sends back after opening a letter. Only they and the letter's author can hear it.
  • A device key: a random code on your phone that lets it into your family space. We keep only a scrambled (hashed) copy.

Amaanat shares one family space with our other family apps, such as Kissa, so a family only joins once.

How the seal works

Our server will not give out a sealed letter's recording, photo or note to anyone except its author until the letter opens. Dated letters open on that date. "Open when…" letters open when their person chooses. Changes that could open a letter sooner, like setting a wedding date or handing a child's letters to them, need the grandparent's okay or a notice period they can undo.

Sealed letters are not sent for speech-to-text.

Where it is stored

On Cloudflare (database D1 and file storage R2), over encrypted connections (HTTPS). Your internet address is used for a moment to stop abuse and is not stored with your letters.

Permissions

  • Microphone: only while you record a letter or a thank-you, after you tap to record.
  • Photos: chosen through Android's own photo picker, so Amaanat sees only the photo you pick.
  • Notifications (optional): to tell you on the day a letter opens.

Deleting

  • Before a letter is opened, its author can record it again or take it back.
  • The person a letter is for can remove it after opening.
  • The family organiser can choose Delete all our family's data, which permanently deletes everything, sealed letters included.

Lost your phone? Use amaanat.helpivy.com/delete or email us.

Reporting

Every opened letter and thank-you has a Report option. You can also email us.

Children

Amaanat is made for adults and their families. Letters for a young child are kept by a parent until the child joins. Children don't have accounts.

Third parties

No advertising, analytics or social SDKs. We share nothing with anyone except Cloudflare, which runs our storage.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here with a new date.
