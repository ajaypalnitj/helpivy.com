Chai Paheli — Privacy Policy

Android app com.helpivy.paheli and paheli.helpivy.com · Effective 8 November 2026

The short version

  • Chai Paheli collects no personal information. No accounts, no ads, no analytics, no trackers.
  • Your progress (days played, chai cups, settings) stays on your phone.
  • The puzzles and their read-aloud audio come with the app and work offline.

Information we collect

None. Chai Paheli does not ask for or collect your name, email, phone number, location, contacts or device identifiers.

Progress on your device

Days played, your chai cups, your language and your settings are saved only on your phone, or in your browser on the website. Uninstalling the app (or clearing the site's data) erases them.

Sharing a riddle

When you tap "Send", Chai Paheli hands a picture of the riddle and a link to WhatsApp or another app that you choose. We don't see who you send it to. Opening a riddle link on paheli.helpivy.com only loads that public riddle.

Recording your own riddle

If you choose to record your own riddle to share, the recording is sent only where you share it. If it is stored on our server so the link can play it, it is kept privately on Cloudflare and anyone with the link can play it. You can delete it from the app at any time.

Permissions

  • Notifications (optional): only if you turn on the daily reminder.
  • Microphone (optional): only while you record your own riddle, after you tap to record.

Website

paheli.helpivy.com is served by Cloudflare. Like any website, Cloudflare briefly processes your internet address to deliver the page and stop abuse. We run no analytics on it.

Children

Chai Paheli is made for adults and families. It is not directed to children and collects nothing from anyone.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here with a new date.

Contact

Questions? Email help@helpivy.com.
