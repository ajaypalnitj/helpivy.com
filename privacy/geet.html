Geet — Privacy Policy

Android app com.helpivy.geet and geet.helpivy.com · Effective 9 November 2026

The short version

  • Geet keeps the songs that matter to your elders and the stories they tell about them. Only your family can see or hear them.
  • We never host, stream or store music or song lyrics. "Play the song" opens YouTube.
  • No email, phone number or password. No ads, no analytics, no trackers. We never sell or share your family's content, and we never copy or imitate anyone's voice.
  • Delete anything you added, or the whole family's data, any time.

What we keep, and why

  • Names you type: your family's name and each member's first name or nickname.
  • Songs: the song title, the life chapter and decade, an optional YouTube link a family member adds, and an optional recording of your elder humming or singing it, which stays private to your family.
  • Stories about each song, voice replies, suggestions, antakshari turns and hearts.
  • A device key: a random code on your phone that lets it into your family space. We keep only a scrambled (hashed) copy.

Geet shares one family space with our other family apps, such as Kissa, so a family only joins once.

YouTube

When you tap "Play the song", Geet opens YouTube (the app or the website) with a search or the link your family chose. What happens on YouTube is covered by Google's privacy policy, not ours. Geet does not send YouTube anything about you or your family.

Turning voice into words

A recording may be sent to a speech-to-text service to make a rough written draft that only your family sees. Today this is Cloudflare Workers AI. If we change provider, this page will name the new one first.

Where it is stored

On Cloudflare (database D1 and file storage R2), over encrypted connections (HTTPS). Your internet address is used for a moment to stop abuse and is not stored with your content.

Permissions

  • Microphone: only while you record a story, a hum or a reply, after you tap to record.

Deleting

  • Delete anything you added.
  • Leave the family from Settings.
  • The family organiser can choose Delete all our family's data, which permanently deletes everything.

Lost your phone? Use geet.helpivy.com/delete or email us.

Reporting

Everything shared in the family has a Report option. You can also email us.

Children

Geet is made for adults and their families. It is not directed to children.

Third parties

No advertising, analytics or social SDKs. We share nothing with anyone except Cloudflare, which runs our storage and speech-to-text.

Reading aloud

"Read this screen to me" plays recordings that come with the app. Names and words your family typed are spoken by your phone's own voice, on your phone. Nothing is sent anywhere to be read aloud.

Telling the family on WhatsApp

After something new is saved, the app may offer to share a link with your family group. It only does this if you tap Share, and you can turn the offer off in Settings. The link opens only for members of your family; anyone else sees nothing, not even your family's name.

One family across our apps on your phone

If you use more than one of our family apps on the same Android phone, they share your family sign-in (a random device key, your family, your language) with each other, so you don't have to join again in each one. Android only lets apps signed by us read it. Other apps can't. The websites don't share it.

Learning what helps

To learn whether the app is helping, our server counts a few things each day, per app: for example how many stories were recorded or games finished. Each count is only a number. No name, family, phone or internet address is stored with it.

In Settings you can answer "Is this helping?". We keep your answer with the app's name, its language and the day, plus only what you choose to add (words, a short voice note, or an email if you'd like a reply). It isn't linked to your name or family.

To stop abuse, we keep a scrambled (keyed hash) form of your internet address together with the day for one day only, then delete it.

Family home, new phones

So the family home can show a dot when something new is waiting, our server remembers when each member last opened each of our family apps. This is deleted when they leave or the family is deleted.

A family member can make a link to set up a grandparent's phone, or to move them to a new phone. Each link works once and expires. Every family member can see who made it, for whom and when. Moving to a new phone signs out the old one. Sealed letters written before the move stay sealed for 14 days.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here with a new date.
