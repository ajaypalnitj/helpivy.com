Table Night

Four board games on one cosy table · Meta Quest 3 and 3S

Game night, any night

Pull up a chair. Carrom, Chess, Ludo and Snakes & Ladders are set out on a real-feeling table, with an opponent across from you who cheers, winces and plays fair. Coming soon to the Meta Horizon Store.

Four games

  • Carrom — slide the striker, aim, hold for power and let go. A guide line shows where your coin will go.
  • Chess — pick pieces up and set them down like real ones. Opponents from gentle to tough, with hints, puzzles and lessons.
  • Ludo — throw the die and race home. Two to four players.
  • Snakes & Ladders — four worlds, with ladders to climb and snakes that move.

Four rooms

A fireside lounge, a library with rain on the window, a garden under a pergola and a rooftop at dusk. Change rooms whenever you like.

Made to be easy

  • Play sitting or standing, in one spot. Nothing moves around you.
  • "How to play" shows each game's controls as short moving pictures.
  • Undo a move, ask for a hint, or pick up a saved game later.
  • Play the computer opponents, or pass the headset around the family.
  • No ads, no accounts, no online play, and it works without the internet.

Help

The table is in the wrong place.

Hold the grip on the table's edge and move it, or press the Menu button and choose Recentre.

How do I learn a game's controls?

Open the folio on the table and choose How to play. It also appears the first time you open each game.

How do I leave or restart a game?

Press the Menu button on the left controller for the Paused card: Resume, Leave game, New game and Settings. Your game is saved when you leave.

Something else?

Email help@helpivy.com and tell us what happened. We usually reply within a few days.
