Read & speak Punjabi, the fun way

Get it onGoogle Play

Free · no ads · no sign-up · works offline

Read Words

Meet the 35 letters (painti akhar) and the vowel signs (matras) one at a time, then build real words like ਘਰ (home), ਸਕੂਲ (school) and ਪਰਿਵਾਰ (family).

Read Sentences

Put words together and read warm, everyday lines out loud: family, food, greetings, colours, animals and feelings.

How it works

  • Tap any letter, word or part to hear it spoken aloud, guided by Sheru the friendly lion.
  • Sound out each part, then hear the whole word or sentence.
  • Quick, friendly quizzes and a daily review help it stick. Earn stars and keep a daily streak.
  • Optional record-and-compare lets a child hear themselves next to the reference voice. It is off by default and audio is never saved or sent.
